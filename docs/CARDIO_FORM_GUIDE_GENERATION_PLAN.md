# Cardio form-guide production plan

## Goal and visual system

Create one in-app, portrait 2:3 instructional card for every cardio catalog
entry. Match the approved mobility cards: warm beige studio, natural window
light, charcoal mat or believable machine floor, fitted unbranded athletic wear,
warm-cream rounded callout boxes, thin gold leader lines, and exactly one gold
motion arrow. The card supplements the app's written steps; it must teach the
main alignment and motion at a glance, rather than repeat every instruction.

Use clean side or three-quarter views and enough empty space that every callout
is readable at the app's 360-point display height. Use a two- or three-stage
ghosted sequence only where the exercise has a distinct start and finish;
otherwise show the clearest working position. Never show an unsafe end range,
reversed direction, ambiguous equipment, logos, watermarks, titles, step
numbers, medical claims, or extra prose.

Use an outdoor setting—not the studio—for walking, stair walking, and other
future naturally outdoor cardio. Keep those cards equally clean and scannable:
a paved park path or quiet outdoor stairs, natural daylight, a simple softly
blurred landscape, and the same cream callouts, gold leader lines, and motion
arrow.

### Model-diversity rotation

Cast a different adult demonstrator for each row in the following rotating
order, while retaining practical athletic clothing and a representative,
non-stereotyped presentation: Black woman, East Asian man, South Asian woman,
white man, Latina woman, Black man, East Asian woman, South Asian man, Middle
Eastern woman, white woman, Latino man, Black nonbinary adult. Repeat that
cycle through the catalog. This provides broadly even gender and racial
representation across the 59 cards without asking any individual to represent
an exercise category.

### Shared generator prefix

Prepend this production specification to each brief below:

> Use case: scientific-educational. Asset type: mobile exercise form-guide
> card. Create a polished photorealistic vertical 2:3 instructional image in
> the established CoachFit mobility-card style: warm beige studio, soft natural
> window light, charcoal floor/mat where appropriate, warm cream rounded
> callout boxes with thin gold leader lines, and exactly one unambiguous gold
> motion arrow. Adult model: [assigned diversity rotation]. Use the exact
> all-caps callout text below, no title or other text. Keep the whole body and
> named equipment visible. No logos, watermark, split-screen UI, anatomical
> overlays, or unsafe form. The arrow must follow the stated primary motion.

## Integration and acceptance review

1. Generate the cards one at a time using the matching brief and the shared
   prefix. Inspect every output before accepting it; regenerate when the exact
   callout text, arrow direction, action, or equipment is wrong.
2. Save approved files in `assets/images/exercises/` using the output names
   above. Do not overwrite existing mobility or third-party stills.
3. Add each card as the first `stills` item in its `EXERCISE_MEDIA` entry using
   `formGuide(require(...))`. This ensures the alignment image displays above
   an existing instructional clip.
4. Review each image at the app's 360-point display height. Reject cards with
   clipped limbs, illegible callouts, duplicated arrows, a reversed side or
   arrow, unsafe bench/machine setup, missing rope/bell/dumbbells, or a
   misleading multi-stage transition.
5. Run the catalog/media tests after wiring the assets and manually open one
   guide from each category: steady machine, bodyweight interval, loaded
   interval, aerobic step, and kickboxing.
