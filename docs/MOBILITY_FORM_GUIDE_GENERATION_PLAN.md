# Remaining mobility form guides

## Shared art direction

Create one portrait, 2:3 in-app instructional card per row. Match the existing
warm beige studio, natural window light, dark charcoal mat, authentic athletic
clothing, warm cream rounded callout boxes, thin gold leader lines, and gold
motion arrow. Use one adult demonstrator, a clean side or three-quarter view,
and generous framing. The card is a quick alignment reference, not the full
how-to: use 2–4 short all-caps callouts plus exactly one clear arrow that names
the primary motion. No title, logo, watermark, step numbers, medical claims,
or extra instructional copy. Show the named equipment exactly as described.

## Integration and review

Save each approved card at its stated output path under `assets/images/exercises/`,
then attach it as `formGuide(...)` in `src/domain/catalog/media.ts`. Review every
card at the app’s 360-point display height: the primary pose, every callout,
the equipment, and the arrow direction must remain legible. Reject and regenerate
any image that reverses a side, points the arrow at a non-moving joint, omits the
named prop, creates unreadable text, or depicts a painful/end-range position.
