# CoachFit — App Store launch kit

Prepared for version 1.0.0. This listing is written from the features currently in the app: adaptive sessions based on goals, recovery, and equipment; live set tracking; exercise swaps; progress and achievements; optional Apple Health write-back; and Live Activities.

## Positioning

CoachFit is the focused personal-training app for people who want a capable plan without a subscription, ads, or a cluttered feed. It should feel calm and premium, not loud or "hardcore": practical coaching for the workout in front of you.

**Primary promise:** A workout that fits today.

**Proof points:** Goals, recovery, and available equipment shape every plan. Track the work set by set, adjust without starting over, and see strength, endurance, volume, and consistency accumulate.

**Purchase promise:** One-time purchase, $3.99. No ads. No subscriptions. No in-app purchases.

### Description

CoachFit gives you a practical workout plan for the day you actually have.

Set your goals, training experience, available equipment, and the areas you want to take care with. CoachFit turns that into a clear session with the right focus, duration, and structure for today. Before you start, a quick check-in on sleep, energy, soreness, and intent helps shape the work ahead—so the plan can fit how you feel, not force you through a fixed routine.

TRAIN WITH A PLAN THAT FITS

Choose strength, cardio, mobility, yoga, stretching, or a balanced session. Set a time target, include the parts you want—warm-up, conditioning, and cooldown—and choose where to focus. CoachFit builds a complete, equipment-aware workout you can review before you begin.

MAKE THE CATALOG YOURS

Explore 364 exercises, search by what you need, save favorites, and exclude movements you do not want suggested. Open form guidance and direct video links when you want a closer look at an exercise. Your preferences are respected when CoachFit builds or adjusts a session.

STAY FOCUSED DURING THE WORKOUT

Move through your session exercise by exercise. Log reps, load, effort, and completed sets; use rest timers; review how-tos and history; and replace an exercise when something doesn’t fit. Your plan stays flexible without losing the flow of the workout.

MAKE PROGRESS VISIBLE

See your training calendar, weekly volume, progressive overload, strength trends, endurance trends, personal records, achievements, and consistency in one place. After each workout, a short check-in helps CoachFit make the next session more useful.

DESIGNED TO STAY OUT OF THE WAY

Use the app in light or dark mode and choose kilograms or pounds. Keep an active workout close by with Live Activities. With your permission, send completed workouts to Apple Health.

ONE PURCHASE. NO INTERRUPTIONS.

CoachFit is a $3.99 one-time purchase. There are no ads, subscriptions, or in-app purchases.

Always train within your limits and seek qualified guidance when needed.

### Visual direction

- Use the app’s dark charcoal palette, warm ivory surfaces, and lime accent as the campaign system. Keep one primary device per image; do not use a busy gym photo background.
- Headline: bold, sentence case, 68–88 px. Supporting line: 36–44 px. Left-align both and use a generous top margin.
- Keep the real app screen at full bleed below the title area. Avoid phone bezels, drop shadows, or tiny “feature list” copy.
- The first three images should sell the core loop—plan, train, adapt—before progress and ownership messaging.
- Use realistic but clearly illustrative data (for example, 20 kg dumbbells, 8 reps, 7/10 effort). Never imply medical advice, guaranteed results, or specific calorie outcomes.

## Capture checklist

1. Prepare an athlete profile with a strength goal, kg/lb chosen deliberately, and a realistic home or gym equipment list.
2. Seed four weeks of completed workouts, including strength and cardio, before capturing Progress.
3. Turn on a single appearance mode for the whole set (dark recommended) and hide personal notifications, battery warnings, and debug overlays.
4. Capture at the highest required iPhone resolution, then make the seven framed creatives from the captures. Apple accepts 1–10 screenshots and will scale the highest-resolution set down to smaller sizes when the UI is the same.
5. Keep the U.S. dollar amount only in the U.S. creative. For other storefronts, use `One purchase. No ads. No subscriptions. No in-app purchases.` and let the store show localized price.
