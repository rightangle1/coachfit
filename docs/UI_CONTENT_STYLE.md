# UI content style

CoachFit speaks like a calm, capable coach: warm, direct, and brief.

## Principles

- Lead with a clear next action: “See my workout”, not “Build today’s session”.
- Use familiar words before training shorthand. Explain RPE as effort at its first use and describe e1RM as an estimated strength value.
- Ask supportive, specific questions: “How are you feeling today?” and “Any areas to take care with?”
- Keep labels short, sentence case, and present tense. Prefer “Save workout” to “Submit debrief”.
- Avoid vague filler, internal language, and overly clinical phrasing. Do not use “build”, “debrief”, “logged”, or “considering” when a plain action is clearer.

## Patterns

| Use | Avoid |
| --- | --- |
| Your equipment | What have you got? |
| How hard did it feel? | Overall effort (RPE) |
| Add an optional note | Notes (optional) |
| Your plan is ready | Continue |
| Pick up where you left off | You’re mid-workout |
